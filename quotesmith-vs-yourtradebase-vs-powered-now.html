QuoteSmith vs YourTradebase vs Powered Now: Which Quoting Tool Wins in 2026?

Choosing a quoting tool is one of those decisions that feels small until you are three jobs deep and still fighting the software instead of sending quotes. The three names UK trades keep coming back to are QuoteSmith, YourTradebase and Powered Now. They overlap, but they are not built for the same job.

This comparison is for the tradesperson who wants a straight answer. We look at what each tool actually does, how fast it gets a professional quote out the door, and who each one suits. By the end you will know exactly which to pick.

Note: features and pricing for the other tools change often, so always confirm current details on their own sites before you sign up. Pricing referenced for QuoteSmith is its live published figure.

Quick verdict

  • Best for fast, focused quoting: QuoteSmith - a branded PDF quote in about two minutes, GBP 19.99/mo, no bloat.
  • Best for an all-in-one job and invoicing system: YourTradebase - more moving parts, more to manage.
  • Best for field-heavy admin on the move: Powered Now - broad feature set aimed at managing the whole business.
  • Skip if: you want one focused tool that just produces a great-looking quote fast - then the heavier all-in-one platforms are more than you need.

YourTradebase

YourTradebase is a well-known UK platform that handles quotes alongside job management, scheduling and invoicing. It aims to be the system you run the whole business through, not just the quoting step.

That breadth is its strength and its trade-off. If you want one place for quotes, jobs and invoices, it covers a lot of ground. If you only want to send a great quote fast, there is more to set up and learn than you strictly need. Confirm current pricing on their site.

Powered Now

Powered Now is another established UK trade platform built around managing the business from the field: quotes, invoices, expenses, scheduling and more, with a strong focus on mobile admin.

Like YourTradebase, it is an all-in-one tool. The upside is everything lives in one app. The downside is that producing a single professional quote means working inside a broader system with more features than a focused quoter. Confirm current pricing on their site.

Deep dive: speed to a finished quote

This is where the three genuinely differ. QuoteSmith is designed so the quote is the whole job: enter the work, get a branded PDF in about two minutes. The all-in-one platforms treat quoting as one feature inside a wider system, so there is more navigation and setup around it.

If your pain is "I lose jobs because my quotes are slow, scrappy or never get sent", a focused quoter removes that pain fastest. Winner: QuoteSmith.

Deep dive: breadth of features

If you want one system to run quotes, jobs, scheduling and invoices, the all-in-one platforms are built for exactly that and a dedicated quoter is not trying to compete there. Winner: YourTradebase and Powered Now, by design.

Deep dive: how much you will actually use

More features are only worth paying for if you use them. Many sole traders and small teams sign up for an all-in-one platform, use the quoting and a slice of the invoicing, and ignore the rest. If that is you, you are paying for breadth you do not touch. A focused tool at GBP 19.99/mo can be the better-value choice precisely because it does not try to do everything. Winner: QuoteSmith for focused users.
